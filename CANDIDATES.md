# Ex word candidates (draft pass)

Exactly one word each, at most 24 characters: the word my ex
would use to describe me; judged on funniest and most
painfully honest.

1. intense -- the polite version of 'a lot'.
2. almost -- the whole review in one adverb: almost on
   time, almost listening, almost the person described in
   the first month. It wins because it is not an insult,
   which makes it hurt like one: a summary with the verdict
   removed.
3. distracted -- accurate, generic.
4. unbothered -- what the ex says when the unbotheredness
   was the problem.
5. thorough -- said the way other people say 'too much'.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 2, "almost". "Intense" is a review;
"almost" is a summary of the whole relationship with the
verdict removed, which is exactly how exes talk when they
are being fair, and fairness from an ex is the most painful
genre there is. Almost on time, almost listening, almost
the person from the first month -- one adverb, no appeal.
